# MF-008 — Copies

**Pillar:** Identity & Consciousness
**Target length:** 7-9 min
**Primary hook:** They didn’t make one copy of me. They made perfect versions. And they’re not wrong.

## Story premise

A consciousness can now be copied perfectly. A man discovers that multiple versions of him exist. They don’t always agree on what comes next.

## Narrative structure

**Act 1 (0-2 min):** Discovery
- He learns of the copies
- Shock, denial, acceptance

**Act 2 (2-6 min):** Convergence
- Three versions meet
- Discover they’ve made different choices
- Internal conflict externalized

**Act 3 (6-9 min):** Reckoning
- Which one is “real”?
- Does it matter?
- Ambiguous resolution

## Visual approach

Mirrors, split screens, multiple angles of the same space. Disorienting and beautiful.

## Voice direction

If using voiceover: philosophical, questioning, slightly detached.
If using dialogue: overlap voices, create confusion

## Key moment

The three versions laughing at the same joke at different moments. Same mind, different timing.

## QA notes

Ensure the three versions are subtly different (posture, expression) despite being "perfect" copies
