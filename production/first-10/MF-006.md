# MF-006 — The Price of Translation

**Pillar:** First Contact
**Target length:** 6-8 min
**Primary hook:** We finally decoded the alien message. We wish we hadn’t.

## Premise

Humanity receives the first communication from extraterrestrial life. But understanding it forces an impossible choice.

## Three-Act Structure

**Act 1:** Discovery and celebration (0-2 min)
- Message arrives
- Teams decode it
- World holds its breath

**Act 2:** Translation and realization (2-6 min)
- Meaning emerges
- Horrifying implication understood
- Characters grapple with knowledge

**Act 3:** Impossible choice (6-8 min)
- Respond or stay silent?
- What does honesty cost?
- Ambiguous ending

## Visual style

Tech noir. Cool lighting, screens, faces illuminated by data. Escalating visual intensity as meaning becomes clear.

## Voice direction

Starts calm (scientist), escalates to urgency (realization). Final moment: quiet acceptance.

## Key constraint

Never show the full message—let viewers imagine it.

## QA checklist

- Verify pacing builds tension
- Ensure final choice is genuinely difficult
- Confirm ending leaves viewers thinking
