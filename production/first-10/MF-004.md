# MF-004 — What If Earth Stopped Spinning for One Second?

**Pillar:** Impossible Earth
**Target length:** 55–70s
**Primary hook:** If Earth stopped spinning for one second, the ground would stop. You would not.

## Script

If Earth stopped spinning for one second, the ground would stop. You would not.

At the equator, Earth’s surface moves at roughly 1,670 kilometres per hour.

If the planet suddenly stopped but the atmosphere, oceans, and everything on the surface kept their momentum, the result would be catastrophic.

Air would continue moving at extreme speed. Oceans would surge across continents. Buildings, vehicles, and people would be thrown eastward.

Then, one second later, if Earth started spinning again just as suddenly, everything would be hit by another violent change in motion.

A gradual slowdown would be very different.

But an instant stop—even for one second—would not feel like a pause. It would feel like the planet had turned into a weapon.
