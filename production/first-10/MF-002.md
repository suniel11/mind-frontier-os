# MF-002: Second Chance

## Metadata
- **Theme:** Choice
- **Duration:** 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Low
- **Tone:** Tense, somber

## Story Premise
Society gives everyone a chance to reset their past. Everyone is given amnesty... except for her.

## Act 1: Hook (0-2 min)
**Visual:** City celebration, people freed from past  
**VO:** "They called it mercy. Universal forgiveness. A chance to begin again. For everyone except me."

## Act 2: Escalation (2-4 min)
- She watches others celebrate freedom
- Discovers why she's excluded
- Internal conflict about her crime/mistake

## Act 3: Resolution (4-5 min)
- She chooses her own redemption path
- Ambiguous but powerful ending
