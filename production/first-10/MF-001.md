# MF-001: The Last Signal

## Metadata
- **Theme:** Consciousness
- **Duration:** 7 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Tone:** Melancholic, hopeful

## Story Premise
After 100 years of silence, a signal arrives from Earth - proof that someone survived. But the message carries an impossible truth.

## Act 1: Hook (0-2 min)
**Visual:** Vast space station, ancient and quiet  
**VO:** "I stopped listening for signals 50 years ago. Then came the day the radio spoke."

## Act 2: Escalation (2-7 min)
- Signal decoded
- Realization: sender is alone
- Emotional weight of connection across time

## Act 3: Resolution (7-10 min)
- Character chooses to respond
- Hope despite distance
- Ambiguous: Will the message arrive in time?

## FLUX Image Prompts

2. "Radio equipment from the past, static patterns, warm lighting, nostalgic, 1080p"

## Voice Direction
- Tone: Introspective, wonder-struck
- Pace: Slow, deliberate
- Emotion arc: Resignation → hope
