# MF-005 — Last Words Before the Void

**Pillar:** Last Moments
**Target length:** 5-7 min
**Primary hook:** They gave her 60 seconds to say goodbye. She used them to say hello.

## Story

A space explorer faces the end of her mission—and her life. Given one final message to Earth, she must choose between closure and curiosity.

## Visual beat sheet

- 0–15s: Lonely spaceship interior
- 15–45s: Memory flashbacks, training days
- 45–2m: The decision moment
- 2m-5m: Her unexpected final message
- 5m-7m: Ambiguous ending

## Voice direction

Quiet, introspective. Strength beneath vulnerability. Pause before revelations.

## Emotional arc

Grief → nostalgia → defiance → hope

## Key visual

Silhouette against Earth rising over the moon’s horizon. Vast, alone, beautiful, heartbreaking.

## FLUX Prompts

1. "Tiny human figure in vast space station, Earth visible through window, bittersweet, cinematic, 1080p"
2. "Hands touching glass, Earth reflection, intimate loneliness, 1080p"

## QA

- Ensure voice emotion arc is consistent
- Verify visual pacing matches emotional beats
- Check that ending is ambiguous but hopeful
