# MF-007 — The Garden in the Ruins

**Pillar:** Survival & Hope
**Target length:** 4-6 min
**Primary hook:** She’s been growing tomatoes in a dead world. One day, they grew back.

## Story

In a post-apocalyptic settlement, a woman tends to a futile garden. Then, against all odds, nature responds.

## Visual journey

- Barren landscape, rust and ash (0-1m)
- Woman working in garden, care and ritual (1-3m)
- Unexpected life, green shoots, color returning (3-4m)
- Final moment: hope or delusion? (4-6m)

## Tone

Quiet, meditative. Beauty amid desolation. Hope without naivety.

## Voice direction

Voiceover optional. If used: internal monologue, matter-of-fact about survival, surprised by hope.

## Ending options

1. Ambiguous: Is this real or has she snapped?
2. Triumphant: Nature is fighting back
3. Bittersweet: One success in an otherwise dead world

## Production notes

Focus on hands, earth, tiny details. Let the camera breathe. Silence is powerful.

## FLUX prompts

"Post-apocalyptic garden, ruins in background, single woman kneeling, green shoots in soil, hope and desolation, cinematic, 1080p"
