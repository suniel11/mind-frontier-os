# MF-009 — Last Breath

**Pillar:** Choice & Sacrifice
**Target length:** 5-7 min
**Primary hook:** She had enough oxygen for two. She chose neither.

## Story

Two astronauts, one oxygen tank. One chooses a third option.

## Act structure

**Setup (0-1.5 min):**
- Oxygen is failing
- Two can breathe if they share
- Both know what that means

**Escalation (1.5-4 min):**
- They debate
- Memories surface
- Fear and love collide

**Resolution (4-7 min):**
- She makes her choice
- Not sacrifice, but something else
- Ambiguous legacy

## Visual palette

Close-ups of faces. Soft focus backgrounds. Intimate and claustrophobic.

## Emotional beats

1. Panic (how do we survive?)
2. Love (I don’t want to lose you)
3. Resignation (one of us dies)
4. Defiance (or neither of us)

## The twist

She doesn’t sacrifice. She chooses presence over survival. The moment becomes about meaning, not mathematics.

## Voice direction

Whispers, in the vacuum. Intimate. Painful.

## QA checklist

- Pacing builds without becoming melodramatic
- Oxygen meter visual doesn’t overshadow emotion
- Ending doesn’t feel like traditional sacrifice arc
