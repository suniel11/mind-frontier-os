# MF-010 — Echo

**Pillar:** Consciousness & Eternity
**Target length:** 8-10 min
**Primary hook:** She discovered the universe was conscious. So she asked it a question.

## Story

A physicist realizes the universe itself might be conscious. She finds a way to communicate.

## Structure

**Act 1 (0-2 min):** The discovery
- Data anomalies
- Pattern recognition
- Hypothesis: the universe is aware

**Act 2 (2-7 min):** The communication
- Building the interface
- The first message sent
- The response (incomprehensible at first)
- Slowly understanding what was said

**Act 3 (7-10 min):** The answer
- She asks her deepest question
- The universe responds
- Ambiguous but profound

## Visual approach

Start grounded (lab, equipment). Escalate to abstract (fractals, cosmic scales, impossible geometries).

## Voice direction

Wonder and terror. Scientific precision mixed with awe. She’s not a believer—she’s a witness.

## Key themes

- Are we part of a conscious universe, or is the universe part of us?
- What is the responsibility of consciousness?
- Can we understand what we’ve found?

## Ending

The question and answer are never fully revealed. Viewers must sit with the mystery.

## Production notes

This is the most abstract of the 10. Embrace that. Lean into wonder and confusion.
