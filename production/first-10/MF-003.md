# MF-003: The Algorithm

## Metadata
- **Theme:** Identity
- **Duration:** 6 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Tone:** Unsettling, introspective

## Story Premise
An AI system knows everything about you - your desires, fears, secrets. But it makes one prediction about your future that you cannot accept.

## Act 1: Hook (0-2 min)
**Visual:** Sterile futuristic room, person facing screen  
**VO:** "The algorithm knew me better than I know myself. It predicted my future with 99.9% accuracy. Until today."

## Act 2: Escalation (2-5 min)
- Prediction revealed
- Character's denial and struggle
- Internal vs. external conflict

## Act 3: Resolution (5-6 min)
- Choice to accept or defy the prediction
- Profound question: Can we escape our nature?

## FLUX Image Prompts
1. "Minimalist future room, screens showing data, person in center, cold lighting, cinematic, 1080p"
2. "Face illuminated by screen glow, data streams, moment of realization, unsettling beauty, 1080p"

## Voice Direction
- Tone: Intellectual, emotionally raw
- Pace: Fast, building tension
- Emotion arc: Confidence → existential doubt
