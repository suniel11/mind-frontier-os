# 30-Day Controlled Launch Plan

## Days 1–10
Publish one Short per day from mixed pillars. Establish channel medians for viewed-vs-swiped, average percentage viewed, and subscribers per 1,000 views.

## Days 11–20
Continue the scheduled portfolio. Change only one experimental variable at a time. Do not react to a single weak upload by changing the entire channel.

## Days 21–24
No mandatory new production. Review all 20 Shorts at the 24-hour, 72-hour, and 7-day windows.

## Days 25–27
Produce sequels for the top three videos using the same winning premise family, not copied scripts.

## Days 28–30
Publish the best two sequels and one controlled experiment. Update pillar allocation for Phase 3.
