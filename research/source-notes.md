# Source Notes & Strategy References

## Intelligence Report Analysis

Phase 1 strategy grounded in comprehensive intelligence report covering:

### Content Pillars
- Survival & Isolation
- Identity & Consciousness  
- Choice & Sacrifice
- First Contact & Connection
- Last Moments & Legacy
- Impossible Earth & Physics

### Publishing Allocation
- 40% Identity/Consciousness themes
- 30% Survival/Choice themes
- 20% First Contact/Mystery themes
- 10% Experimental/Experimental hybrid themes

### Title Systems
- Question format: "What if [premise]?"
- Consequence format: "[Character] had one choice"
- Mystery format: "The [noun] nobody talks about"
- Isolation format: "Last [noun] on [location]"

### Retention Framework
- Open strong: Hook within 10 seconds
- Build tension: Escalate stakes gradually
- Peak: Emotional or conceptual crescendo
- Resolution: Ambiguous but satisfying

### Authenticity Controls
- No AI watermarks or signature effects
- Grounded sci-fi (no fantasy elements)
- Scientific accuracy checked before production
- Emotional truth prioritized over spectacle
