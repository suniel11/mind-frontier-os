# Editorial Bible - Mind Frontier

## Three-Act Structure (Standard)
- **Act 1 (0-2 min):** Setup + hook
- **Act 2 (2-7 min):** Escalation + crisis
- **Act 3 (7-10 min):** Resolution or ambiguity

## Story Requirements
- [ ] Opening hook within 10 seconds
- [ ] Emotional stakes established
- [ ] Sci-fi element central to plot
- [ ] Satisfying or thought-provoking ending

## Content Pillars
1. **Survival Stories** - Characters against odds
2. **Identity Stories** - Who are we?
3. **Choice Stories** - Defining moments
4. **Consciousness Stories** - What does it mean to think?

## Do's ✅
- Respect audience intelligence
- Show consequences
- Use visual storytelling
- End with meaningful ambiguity

## Don'ts ❌
- Explain the sci-fi element
- Use cheap jump scares
- Info-dump backstory
- Sacrifice story for effects
