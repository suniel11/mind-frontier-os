# Brand Bible - Mind Frontier

## Vision
Mind Frontier explores what it means to be human in worlds that challenge everything we know.

## Core Themes
1. **Identity** - Who are we when our world changes?
2. **Choice** - What defines us in impossible moments?
3. **Survival** - Not just living, but why we choose to live

## Voice & Tone
- Philosophical, not preachy
- Intimate, not distant
- Mysterious, not confusing
- Hopeful, even in darkness

## Target Audience
- Age: 18-45
- Interests: Sci-fi, philosophy, storytelling, AI
- Values: Intelligent narratives, emotional depth, visual beauty

## Visual Identity
- Resolution: 1080p (16:9)
- Color Palette: Cinematic, grounded sci-fi
- Tone: Serious, immersive, beautiful
- No cartoonish or grayscale aesthetics
