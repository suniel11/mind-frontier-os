# Visual Style Guide - Mind Frontier

## FLUX Prompt Formula

`[TIME OF DAY] [LOCATION] [SUBJECT] [ACTION] [MOOD], [TECHNICAL SPEC], cinematic, grounded sci-fi`

## Example

"Dusk in abandoned city, lone figure standing before massive AI structure, looking uncertain, moody atmospheric lighting, cinematic, grounded sci-fi, 1080p"

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** Cool blues, deep grays
- **Accent:** Warm amber, soft gold
- **Avoid:** Neon, oversaturation, cartoon effects

## Composition Guidelines
- Shallow depth of field (character focus)
- Symmetry for establishment shots
- Asymmetry for emotional moments
- Wide shots for scale and isolation

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Overly detailed backgrounds
- ❌ Unrealistic lighting
- ❌ Cartoon or anime aesthetics
- ❌ Cluttered frames
- ✅ Clean, purposeful, cinematic
